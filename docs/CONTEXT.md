# Glossary

Terms as this repo uses them. One term, one meaning.

## Service

- **Definition:** One of the ten top-level categories every estimate task line and logged actual is grouped under: Strategy, Design, Content Creation, Social Media Marketing, Paid Social, SEM, SEO, Digital, PR & Communications, Event Management.
- **Boundary:** A reporting grouping, not a team and not a Harvest task. Harvest task names map to a Service; they are not Services themselves.

## Sub-task

- **Definition:** The second level under a Service, e.g. Digital > Integration.
- **Boundary:** Not a ClickUp subtask and not a line in an estimate. It is a tag applied to a line.

## Archetype

- **Definition:** A named job shape at small, medium or large size, carrying human-approved hours by Service and a typical sell-price band.
- **Boundary:** Reviewed and approved by a person. Distinct from an anchor, which sizes one kind of work rather than a whole job.

## Anchor

- **Definition:** A starting hour range for one kind of work, held in `job-estimator/estimate-anchors.md`.
- **Boundary:** A curated conclusion, not evidence. Real job history always wins over an anchor.

## Estimate Calibration doc

- **Definition:** The ClickUp doc holding one record per finished job — what it was estimated at and what it took.
- **Boundary:** Evidence only. Curated figures (anchors, archetypes, rates, taxonomy) live in git, never here.

## Department split

- **Definition:** Estimated hours grouped by Service, showing how the work is resourced.
- **Boundary:** Presentation only. It never adjusts the hours, and it is not a measure of anyone's speed.

## Price sense-check

- **Definition:** Comparing an estimate's implied dollar value against the archetype's typical sell price, to catch hours that have drifted somewhere unquotable.
- **Boundary:** A check, not a quote. It never puts a dollar figure against a task line.
